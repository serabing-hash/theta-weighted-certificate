# Primary sources and their precise scope

- Ruizhe Zhang, Optimal Extrapolation Bounds for Sparse Fourier Sums, arXiv:2607.10501v2, 13 October 2026. https://arxiv.org/html/2607.10501v2 . Theorem 1.1 and Proposition 3.7 use complex coefficients and real Fourier frequencies. Proposition 3.7 holds for every nonnegative extrapolation distance. Section 3.1 is the weighted elimination and energy-concentration method adapted here. Appendix A, including Remark A.7, discusses the classical all-pass basis construction. The present strip/full-prefix theta theorem is not a theorem stated in that source.
- Tamás Erdélyi, Inequalities for exponential sums, Sbornik: Mathematics 208(3) (2017), 433–464, DOI 10.1070/SM8670. https://doi.org/10.1070/SM8670 . Author manuscript dated 8 November 2016: https://people.tamu.edu/~terdelyi/papers-online/sbornik150R.pdf . Relevant background on uniform exponential-sum inequalities. The proof in the present manuscript does not invoke its half-line derivative theorem as an unproved input.
- Elchin Hasanalizade, Quanli Shen, Peng-Jie Wong, Counting zeros of the Riemann zeta function, arXiv:2107.06506v1, 14 July 2021. https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.06506v1 . Corollary 1.2 is the explicit count quoted in Section 8. Its argument-principle count includes multiplicities. The published finite-height proof uses numerical zero verification. The main asymptotic argument can instead use the classical O(log T) count error.
- H. von Mangoldt, Zur Verteilung der Nullstellen der Riemannschen Funktion, Mathematische Annalen 60 (1905), 1–19. https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01447494 . Historical Riemann–von Mangoldt count source.
- NIST Digital Library of Mathematical Functions. https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.9 , https://dlmf.nist.gov/20.7 , https://dlmf.nist.gov/25.4 , https://dlmf.nist.gov/25.10 . Classical Gamma, theta and zeta identities. These pages do not supply this manuscript's theta-envelope constants or complete prime-loss bound; those are proved directly.

All source-scope statements were checked against primary texts. No earlier manuscript by the author is used as a bibliography entry. The body transparently identifies the form normalization and carrier comparison as part of preceding stages of the same research and rederives the needed formulas.
