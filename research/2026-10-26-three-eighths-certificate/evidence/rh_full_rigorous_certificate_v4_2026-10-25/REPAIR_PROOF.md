# Remove the old NumPy power arithmetic premise

The old baseline H assembly used NumPy hh**3, but its stated gamma model referred only to classical binary64 arithmetic. No observed numerical error is asserted. Rather than infer historical correctness from a current runtime test, v4 replaces every old smooth functional by a new enclosure whose cubic coefficient is explicitly hh*hh*hh/6. The h values are exact normal dyadic powers of two, and existing coefficient-radius gamma12 terms bound multiplication/division and input-radius coupling.

Let Q0 be the exact original 58-panel midpoint Taylor functional, and Delta the exact signed patch new88-old16. V4 proves ||Q0-Hsafe|| <= e_safe. The saved v3 point certificate proves ||Delta-Delta_mid|| <= e_patch independently of old Hmid and old H point/BLAS arithmetic. Therefore Hsafe+Delta_mid encloses Q0+Delta with e_safe+e_patch plus new matrix-addition/symmetry error. This is exactly the same 130-panel composite functional. Its integration remainder is sum q_i/k_i^4, bounded by the existing exact-Fraction plan. Parent q_i were computed with explicit outward multiplication (including the v1 candidate used by v2's minimum), not NumPy power.

The theta correction and analytic endpoint caps are bounds on physical-domain integrals and are unchanged by midpoint-panel refinement. Their accumulation occurs separately from the old midpoint hh**3 code. Scaling their old error by Dmax squared is valid. None of the old H midpoint center, point/input error, BLAS error, symmetrization error, or coefficient-radius repair is used.

The fine Gmid/Grad and candidate interval certificates are reused, but their scaled congruence is newly evaluated. If eta bounds ||Uexact D-Ustored D||, Un bounds ||Uexact D||, Uf bounds the stored Frobenius norm, dGfine bounds the fine entry-radius matrix norm, and Gn bounds ||Gmid||, then G uncertainty <= Un^2*dGfine+(2*Uf*eta+eta^2)*Gn. Two BLAS products, symmetrization and underflow receive the same audited classical arithmetic bounds. H sum/symmetry and final G-H subtraction/symmetry receive new explicit bounds.

All terms are nonnegative outward sums: new G input and BLAS errors; new baseline point/input/BLAS/symmetry; saved patch point/input/BLAS/symmetry; H sum/symmetry; theta/caps; composite quadrature; final subtraction/symmetry. No v1/v3 F center is reused. The exact source, arbitrary dyadic projection trial, original moment constraints, and D congruence are unchanged. The independently verified inertia helper remains conditional only on the supplied total Loewner premise, which this pipeline constructs.

Runtime hashes are pre/post evidence of the exact implementation loaded; they do not replace the arithmetic proof. Successful fixed-matrix certification is not a proof of the whole RH parameter family.
