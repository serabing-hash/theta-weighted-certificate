# Two-mode theta positivity: analytic research support

23 October 2026. This is a research-support package, not the manuscript or its TeX/PDF source.

## Reading order and results

1. [PHYSICAL_PROOF.md](PHYSICAL_PROOF.md): full, unscaled theta source; complete Gamma and all-prime-power budgets; actual positive cross sign; lifted six-vertex proof of b-c²/a > 1/62500 on [14,29/2].
2. [ORIGINAL_DOMAIN.md](ORIGINAL_DOMAIN.md): common-cutoff minimum-domain admission, operator-null source space, full polarized projection identity G_projected = G_inv + 2zzᵀ, and coefficient bound > 1/64000.
3. [NEARBY_FREQUENCIES.md](NEARBY_FREQUENCIES.md): explicit anchored radius δ, complete weighted form continuity, divided-difference seam, projected extension and gap²/384000 original-coordinate bound.
4. [CENTERED_RADIUS.md](CENTERED_RADIUS.md): quadratic error in central coordinates, explicit full-gap radius Δ > 100δ on the overlapping frequency domain, and gap²/256000 original-coordinate bound. The midpoint must be in the band; outward endpoint pairs are not covered by this stronger comparison.
5. [SIGNED_CROSS_FREQUENCY.md](SIGNED_CROSS_FREQUENCY.md): exact signed two-channel identities, all-real-half-gap prime remainder < 242/10^6 for in-band midpoint, determinant cancellation, and precise unresolved nonlocal estimates.

[VERIFICATION.md](VERIFICATION.md) distinguishes review scopes. [REPRODUCTION.md](REPRODUCTION.md) gives analytic reading and byte-verification instructions. [REFERENCES.md](REFERENCES.md) records classical inputs and the immutable older support dependency.

No theorem here establishes arbitrary separated-pair positivity, actual zeta-root-pair applicability, root simplicity, arbitrary-rank/global positivity, or RH. Neither explicit radius has been numerically evaluated. A positive pole-square gain is not claimed.
