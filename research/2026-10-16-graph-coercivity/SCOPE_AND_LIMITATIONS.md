# Exact scope and limitations

## Included result

A local theorem for the fixed B=3/4 complex L2 finite-window graph: pointwise fiber coercivity, nonnegative position and complete-tail reserves, a closed auxiliary form, the full-space inverse bound, complex-linear reflection, and one inclusion from a minimal Gamma-energy completion into the auxiliary weighted form domain.

## Explicitly not established

- The Riemann Hypothesis, global positivity, all-low positivity or full Schur positivity.
- Identification with an original minimum-domain realization, sector-to-core extension or shell transfer.
- Full-pole correction, low16 constrained inverse, or replacing a constrained inverse by a simple compression.
- A physical Q identity, b_full identity, or Legendre diagonalization. The portions of the Max review relying on those inherited premises are excluded from the adopted proof.
- A fixed positive mu_0 borrowed from elsewhere. The valid resolvent statement is for any explicitly chosen mu>=0.
- A practical endpoint-jet improvement, a new residual sign or an updated numerical certificate.
- Optimality of c_coer, novelty, or extension to other B. In particular 2log(3)-log(2)>2B has a small margin at the fixed B.

## Evidence distinctions

Hashes establish supplied-byte identity, not mathematical correctness, authorship, originality, or historical timestamps. Historical source statements about calculations are not fresh execution receipts. Model-generated reviews contain arguments worth checking; their labels and agreement are not proof.

The full source texts contain broader research that is deliberately not republished here. The excerpts may retain source equation numbers, notation and limited historical cross-references. Those cross-references do not expand the adopted theorem. The positive constant is named c_coer in the adopted statement; the Max excerpt's original kappa_g label is a documented notation collision, not a second constant to be added.

The exact special-a fraction is preserved as an optional source ledger, and the Claude review explicitly did not recompute its full numerator. No conclusion in the general-a local theorem depends on it.
