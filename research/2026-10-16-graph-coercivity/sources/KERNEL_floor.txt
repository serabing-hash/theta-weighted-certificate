## Original lines 114–153

114: ## 3. Direct proof of the proposed kernel floor
115: 
116: Set x=s/2. Algebra gives
117: 
118:     rho(s)=[csch x+sech x]/4,
119:     k(s)=[csch x-1/x+sech x]/4.                    (6)
120: 
121: We prove the two proposed auxiliary inequalities rather than assume
122: them. Absolutely convergent power series give, for every x>0,
123: 
124:     (1-x^2/6)sinh x
125:       =x-sum_(n=2)^infinity
126:            [(2n)(2n+1)-6]x^(2n+1)/[6(2n+1)!] < x,
127: 
128:     (1-x^2/2)cosh x
129:       =1-sum_(n=2)^infinity
130:            [(2n)(2n-1)-2]x^(2n)/[2(2n)!] < 1.
131: 
132: The first omitted correction in each product is exactly zero, and
133: every displayed summand is positive. Dividing by positive sinh x
134: and cosh x gives
135: 
136:     csch x > 1/x-x/6,
137:     sech x > 1-x^2/2.                             (7)
138: 
139: For 0<x<=3/4, the polynomial 1-x/6-x^2/2 is decreasing. Equations
140: (6),(7) therefore imply
141: 
142:     k(s) > (1-x/6-x^2/2)/4
143:           >=(1-1/8-9/32)/4=19/128.               (8)
144: 
145: We use the non-strict floor in all form inequalities, including at
146: the zero function. The continuous extension is k(0)=1/4. Also
147: sinh x>=x and cosh x>=1 imply k(s)<=1/4 on this internal interval.
148: Consequently, with c=19/128 and r(s)=k(s)-c,
149: 
150:     0<=r(s)<=13/128,  0<=s<=3/2.                  (9)
151: 
152: No claim about k(s)>=0 outside this interval is made or used. Every
153: external Gamma tail is retained in its original complete form.
