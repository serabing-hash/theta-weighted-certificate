92: ## 3. A complete-tail logarithmic reserve
93: 
94: Direct integration gives
95: 
96:     R(s)=artanh(exp(-s/2))+atan(exp(-s/2)), R'(s)=-rho(s).
97: 
98: We first prove k decreases throughout the required internal distances.
99: Set z=s/2 and a=z^2. For 0<z<=3/4, coefficientwise factorial bounds give
100: 
101:     sinh(z)/z<=1/(1-z^2/6), cosh(z)>=1+z^2/2,
102:     (1+a/2)(1-a/6)^2-1=a(12-10a+a^2)/72>0.
103: 
104: Indeed (2j+1)!>=6^j and 0<a<=9/16; the final numerator is positive there.
105: It follows that z^2 cosh(z)>sinh(z)^2. Hence both csch(z)-1/z and sech(z)
106: have negative derivative. The identity
107: 
108:     k(s)=[csch(z)-1/z+sech(z)]/4
109: 
110: proves k'(s)<0 on (0,3/2]. For 0<x<B this yields
111: 
112:     V_full'(x)=rho(B-x)-rho(B+x)>=x/(B^2-x^2).
113: 
114: Integration and evenness prove the uniform full-tail bound
115: 
116:     V_full(x)>=V0+W_B(x),
117:     W_B(x)=-1/2 log(1-x^2/B^2).                                  (3)
118: 
119: No exterior tail was truncated. In particular
120: 
121:     R(s)=-1/2 log s+log2+pi/4+o(1) as s decreases to zero,
122:     V_full(x)=-1/2 log(B-|x|)+log2+pi/4+R(3/2)+o(1).
123: 
124: The potential therefore has its full logarithmic boundary divergence.
125: It is finite in the open interval and locally bounded there.

127: ## 4. Exact component direct integral, with no missing region
128: 
129: Write r0=B-t, v=u-t, w=2t-u. Fresh positive-series logarithm bounds give
130: 
131:     693/1000<t<694/1000, 1098/1000<u<1099/1000,
132:     2u-t>2B, 4t-u>2B, t+u>2B, 3t-u>B,
133:     0<r0<w<v<t<B, 2t<2B<log5.
134: 
135: These inequalities can be checked with the rational series in Section 5.
136: Up to a finite set of endpoints, use the base intervals
137: 
138:     J=(-r0,r0), I=(u+B-2t,2t-B), K0=(B+t-u,u-B).
139: 
140: Their vertex maps, all with Jacobian one, are:
141: 
142:     seven: x, x+t, x-t, x+v, x-v, x+w, x-w        (x in J);
143:     path:  a-t, a, a-u, a-u+t                    (a in I);
144:     pair:  y-t, y                               (y in K0).
145: 
146: Seven-component edges are
147: 
148:     x--x+t, x--x-t                               weight c2;
149:     x+t--x-v, x-t--x+v                           weight c3;
150:     x-v--x+w, x+v--x-w                           weight c2;
151:     x+t--x-t                                    weight c4.
152: 
153: The path has successive weights c2,c3,c2; the pair has weight c2.
154: To verify that these are the actual complete components, partition (0,B)
155: into the consecutive bands
156: 
157:     (0,r0), (r0,3t-u-B), (3t-u-B,B+t-u), (B+t-u,u-B),
158:     (u-B,u+B-2t), (u+B-2t,2t-B), (2t-B,B).
159: 
160: They respectively contain the central seven vertex, inward path vertex,
161: seven w vertex, pair vertex, seven v vertex, path n=3 endpoint, and seven
162: t vertex. The negative bands are their reflections. All n=4 edges have
163: midpoint in J; all n=2 triples have middle vertex in J. An n=3 upper
164: endpoint belongs to (u-B,B); outside its seven bands it lies exactly in I.
165: Its two inward n=2 neighbors are the stated path ends. The remaining bands
166: are precisely K0 and K0-t. Testing the remaining shifts against the displayed
167: interval endpoints uses 2u-t>2B, 4t-u>2B and t+u>2B, and gives no exits.
168: There is no residual positive-measure isolated band. Any isolated null
169: endpoints are immaterial; the estimate also holds on an isolated component.
170: 
171: Consequently translation evaluation defines a unitary identification
172: 
173:     L2(Omega)=L2(J;C^7) direct_sum L2(I;C^4) direct_sum L2(K0;C^2). (4)
174: 
175: Under (4), multiplication by V_full is the corresponding diagonal, and P_B
176: is the direct integral of these fixed Hermitian adjacency matrices. This
177: assertion is about quadratic forms and L2 representatives, not point values
178: of an abstract equivalence class: translations preserve null sets and all
179: edge products are integrable by Cauchy-Schwarz. Every undirected edge gives
180: 2Re(c conjugate(f_i)f_j), counting the two arithmetic directions once.
181: 
182: Reflection maps parameters x to -x, a to u-a, and y to t-y, respectively,
183: and permutes the vertex labels. All base intervals and weights used below
184: are preserved. Thus the full complex estimate restricts to the even space
185: without identifying complex reflection with conjugation.

187: ## 5. Joint weighted comparison and its positive remainder
188: 
189: For positive vertex weights d_i and actual edge coefficient c>0,
190: 
191:     c[(d_j/d_i)|z_i|^2+(d_i/d_j)|z_j|^2
192:                             -2Re(conjugate(z_i)z_j)]
193:                  =c d_i d_j |z_i/d_i-z_j/d_j|^2>=0.                (5)
194: 
195: This handles every complex phase. Use upper bounds c2<=1/2, c3<=13/20,
196: c4<=7/20 only to bound the row sums in this identity. On seven components,
197: assign weights (center,t,v,w)=(10/9,1,9/10,1/2). The respective row bounds are
198: 
199:     dbar(x)=9/10, 2683/1800, 1, 9/10.                             (6)
200: 
201: On paths assign weight3/5 to degree-one vertices and weight1 to degree-two
202: vertices. Their row bounds are 5/6 and19/20. Pairs use weight1 and row1/2.
203: The function dbar on the corresponding physical bands is even.
204: 
205: Only two nonzero potential reserves are needed. For 0<z<1,
206: 
207:     2 sum_(j=0)^(N-1) z^(2j+1)/(2j+1)
208:       <log((1+z)/(1-z))
209:       <the same sum+2z^(2N+1)/[(2N+1)(1-z^2)].
210: 
211: At (z,N)=(1/3,3),(1/2,4), these prove the bounds for t,u in Section 4;
212: the exact bounding fractions are 842/1215,23581/34020 and
213: 7379/6720,33223/30240. The coefficient bounds follow by additionally
214: using 1/sqrt2<71/100 and 1/sqrt3<7/12. The first three positive terms for
215: log5 with z=2/3 give log5>1924/1215>3/2.
216: 
217: Since t>69/100 and u>109/100, the same positive logarithm series gives
218: 
219:     W_B(2t-B)>1/2 log(625/184)
220:         >317214828/529475129>1063/1800,
221:     W_B(u-B)>(1/2)(34/75)^2=578/5625>1/10.                       (7)
222: 
223: The last margins are 8154628273/953055232200 and31/11250. The seven t
224: vertices have |coordinate|>=2t-B, and the v vertices have |coordinate|>=u-B.
225: All path degree-two vertices are n=3 endpoints with |coordinate|>u-B.
226: Equations (6),(7) therefore prove, at every nonendpoint coordinate,
227: 
228:     eta(x):=W_B(x)+9/10-dbar(x)>=0.                               (8)
229: 
230: For central seven and w vertices eta=W_B; for t vertices it is
231: W_B-1063/1800, for v vertices W_B-1/10. Path leaves give W_B+1/15,
232: path interiors W_B-1/20, and pairs W_B+2/5. Thus eta is positive almost
233: everywhere, may vanish at the single point0, and has a logarithmic divergence
234: at the support boundary. Its piecewise definition includes every component.
235: 
236: Integrating (5) and using (3),(8) proves (1). More precisely the actual form
237: 
238:     J_graph(f)=integral(V_full-V0+9/10)|f|^2-P_B(f,f)              (9)
239: 
240: is nonnegative and satisfies J_graph(f)>=integral eta|f|^2. There is an exact
241: positive decomposition: edge squares from (5), plus the diagonal
242: V_full-V0+9/10-d_actual, where d_actual is the actual weighted row sum.
243: Its difference from eta is V_full-V0-W_B+dbar-d_actual>=0.
244: 
245: The complete potential is retained in (9); W_B is used only to certify a
246: lower bound. Large prime-graph coupling cannot be placed wholly at V0:
247: vertices participating in the large rows simultaneously pay (7). A function
248: supported only at central vertices has no such edges. Equations (4)-(9)
249: make this incompatibility quantitative without an external C_best value.
