# Scope, inherited sources, and unresolved obligations

## Three distinct levels

- Displayed-source theorem: at B=3/4, boundedness/uniform tails on fixed low16 and compactness on each H^s_even, s>0. These conclusions are proved for the explicit formula in ACCEPTED_RESULTS.md.
- Conditional original fixed-supported-sector application: the complete low Schur inequality must first be certified, and the actual mixed-source identity, original minimum high-domain admissibility/comparison, and shell transfer must hold. No such numerical all-coefficient sign is provided here.
- Original whole-line operator: weighted realization, correct topology, a genuine form core or justified support exhaustion, and the separate global criterion are outside the proved result. Status remains unresolved; no RH inference is made.

The graph domain D_g∩X is an auxiliary closed form domain. It must not be silently equated to the original minimum high Gamma domain. Trial columns in the residual identity require the operator domain D(H_X), which is a stricter issue than membership in the form domain D(𝔥_X).

## Conditions not discharged by compactness

1. The actual identity Q(q,h)=〈q,Yh〉 on the original admissible low/high decomposition, with the same displayed source and physical normalizations.
2. Admissibility of q=g−Π_Lg in the original minimum high domain.
3. The high comparison leading to the selected closed auxiliary operator and response form, including the extra form order needed for the sharp response constant.
4. Complete low Q matrix, source head/tail certificates, full-pole filter errors, admissible trial, residual Gram, shell budget and the simultaneous positive matrix sign.
5. The original weighted space H_original=L²_even(κ C_weight dx), C_weight=2cosh(x/2), positive smooth κ, and ℓ(h/κ)=Q(h) on actual original tests. In particular ||h/κ||²_Horiginal=∫|h|² C_weight/κ dx is not automatically ||h||²_L².
6. Appropriate semiboundedness and form topology. A proposed ℓ[f]+||f||² need not be a positive form norm without the required lower bound and correct Hilbert norm.
7. A genuine form core, closure, or justified support-exhaustion argument. Increasing B introduces additional arithmetic shifts and changes certified approximation ranges. Plain L² density is insufficient. A B-uniform strictly positive gap is one possible strategy, not a necessary condition for every nonnegativity transfer.
8. The independent global criterion linking the original operator to RH.

## Corrections controlling this archive

The proof uses a weighted Legendre derivative of W_Bg; ordinary H² regularity of W_Bg is not asserted for general clamped H² inputs. Full-source fast rates cannot be read from only the clamped residual. The exact regular kernel has the distributional −h/24 term in its second derivative. All adjoints and physical Gram metrics are explicit. The full pole is paid exactly once. J=0 remains allowed, isotropic budgets remain valid, and no proxy numerical ratio establishes their success or failure.
