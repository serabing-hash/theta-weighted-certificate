# Unevaluated follow-up questions

The fixed model loss audit distinguishes norm/Young/P_up allowances, primitive enclosure and rounding allowances, and the true directional inverse-response gap. Saved first-action Gram records can support directional bounds, but do not identify all signed action correlations or the actual inverse-response loss. No dominant loss term was numerically measured in this publication.

A possible next bounded investigation is a tau-only screen at the current C and v, with delta, spaces, approximants, P_up and physical budgets unchanged. It would use scalar primitive contractions and the tau dependence of the majorant. It has not been run, no successful tau has been found, and no expectation of success is asserted. A one-direction opening would not certify full372 positivity; a fixed-C failure over tau would not exclude all pairs(C',tau).

Directional inverse-response bounds and genuine trial expansion are other untested possibilities requiring additional information or proofs. This note supplies no new theorem or numerical result for those follow-ups. It does not propose reoptimizing C within the already excluded fixed model or merely removing its final pad.
