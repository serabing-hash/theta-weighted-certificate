## Chosen representation and exact invariant

For each of the same 18 integers n, theta = log(n)/16 is real and the exact
rho = exp(i theta) has Euclidean modulus 1. Form its finite complex enclosure R
with the existing validated 160-bit primitives. R itself need not consist of
unit-modulus points; only the particular enclosed exact rho has that property.

At step k keep an exact dyadic complex center

  q_k = (c_Re,k + i c_Im,k) / 2^140

and a NONNEGATIVE INTEGER E_num,k proving

  |rho^k - q_k|_2 <= E_num,k / 2^140.

Start q_0 = 1 and E_num,0 = 0. For every k > 0:

1. Compute P_k = q_(k-1) R with the native working precision STILL 160 bits.
2. Apply the actual inspected v2_support.rec(P.real,140) and rec(P.imag,140).
   Denote their exact integer records (c_Re,r_Re), (c_Im,r_Im).
3. Construct q_k exactly from c_Re/2^140 + i c_Im/2^140. Verify q_k is finite and
   is_exact(); if the center cannot be represented exactly at 160 bits, HOLD.
4. Set E_num,k = E_num,k-1 + r_Re + r_Im with exact integer addition.
5. Use arb((c_Re,-140),(E_num,k,-140)) as the cosine inclusion.

This is ONE selected implementation of the centered-disc proof. No block
reseeding or alternate algorithm is proposed. The denominator-140 center
rounding is deliberate and its entire discrepancy is charged to E; it does not
change the 160-bit native arithmetic setting.

## Proof and audit of the local serializer

The actual Work helper v2_support.py lines128–139 obtains an exact native
midpoint dyadic, floors it at the requested denominator, computes the outward
absolute upper bound of the difference between the WHOLE input ball and the
chosen dyadic, then rounds the resulting radius upward to an integer.
Consequently each returned record contains the complete corresponding component
of P_k, not just its midpoint. Finite checks precede conversion.

Because q_(k-1) rho belongs to P_k, the local Euclidean discrepancy satisfies

  |q_(k-1)rho - q_k|_2
      <= (r_Re+r_Im) / 2^140.

The coordinate L1 error upper bound is conservative for Euclidean norm. Thus

  |rho^k-q_k|_2
    <= |rho| |rho^(k-1)-q_(k-1)|_2 + |q_(k-1)rho-q_k|_2
    <= (E_num,k-1+r_Re+r_Im) / 2^140.

The old error propagates with factor exactly 1. Seed uncertainty, product
rounding and center quantization are inside the local records. Correlation among
uses of R is harmless: this is a deterministic enclosure proof, not an
independence model. Integer summation cannot round inward. No previous radius
is discarded. Do not normalize q_k or assert it has modulus 1.

Only q_k and E_num,k are recurrence state. Multiplying the rectangle inflated by
E_num,k forward would reintroduce the original wrapping defect. A rectangle may
be constructed from the disc only for cosine accumulation/output.
