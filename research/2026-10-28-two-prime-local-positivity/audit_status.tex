A separate saved-data check passed using Python rational arithmetic and fixed 512-bit outward dyadic rounding, without importing or rerunning the producer and without python-flint. It independently factors the stored interval matrix $F$ by LDL. It also chooses the midpoint matrix $Z$ of the saved inverse-factor enclosure as a rational unit-lower preconditioner, encloses $ZFZ^{\mathsf T}$, and obtains a strictly positive Gershgorin lower bound $d_G$. This implies $F\succeq(d_G/\norm{Z}_{\mathrm F}^2)I$ and, by the same full-norm conversion, again proves a gap greater than $1/20000000000000$. Here $\norm{Z}_{\mathrm F}$ denotes the Frobenius norm of $Z$. The chosen preconditioner need not be assumed to equal the true inverse factor. Residual intervals for the original LDL and inverse contain zero as consistency diagnostics; those diagnostics alone would not prove exact factor identities. The portable checker changes only provenance and file-location checks from the original saved-data audit; its mathematical arithmetic body is unchanged. Its successful report is included in the package.
