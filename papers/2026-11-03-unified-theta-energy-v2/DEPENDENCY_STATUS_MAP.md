1. **Identity:** source/output SHA-256 manifests bind bytes, not mathematical truth.
2. **Artifact:** standalone TeX build, PDF text/structure checks and every-page rendering check production and layout.
3. **Exact scalar algebra:** retained 101 response checks, 28 PNT checks and 31 additional integration checks, plus exact exterior synthetic-model identities. These do not regenerate theta/prime/source enclosures.
4. **Finite stored-data signs:** independent exact matrix reconstruction and integer congruence checks prove statements for the supplied finite matrices. The public companion carries its own input scope and manifest.
5. **Source generation and replay:** the corrected a2048 chain regenerates all dependent source enclosures and finite gates with the arithmetic repair. Historical same-code replay is reproduction, not resolution of a shared primitive defect. Neither layer is a second complete implementation or formal proof.
6. **Analytical review:** scoped review verifies indicated custom dependencies and applications. The entire monograph is not advertised as newly independently audited line by line.

The public package's release manifest establishes which evidence files are actually available. Historical owner-private links and internal transfer records are not presented as public reproduction links.
