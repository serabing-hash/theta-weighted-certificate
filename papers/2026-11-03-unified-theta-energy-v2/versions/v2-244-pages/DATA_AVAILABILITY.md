recorded evidence. It is **not** a claim that every historical raw archive has
been restored, regenerated or redistributed in this version.

The compressed matrix packet extracts the mathematical fields needed by its
checkers; it is not a copy of every original producer JSON file. Original whole
file hashes identify that extraction's provenance. The exact frozen a2048
candidate, by contrast, is provided in full with lossless compression and a
decompression identity check.

The omitted diagnostic floating-solve archive is not needed to regenerate
source components from the authenticated frozen candidate. Creating a new
diagnostic proposal is a different experiment with its own identity and
validation obligations. Do not replace expected reference hashes to make a
different execution appear to match.
