Abstract
We develop an integrated closed-form and spectral framework for the even theta-weighted energy containing the complete Gamma difference integral and every prime power. The maximal energy domain is identified with its weighted physical realization. Exact radicals, compact spectral reductions, weighted synthesis, and full-multiplicity zero-jet form and graph cores isolate the remaining sign problem. Quantitative variance bounds are derived from clearly separated local-sign and complete-response premises. A dimension-independent residual gate and full-norm continuation retain actual source coordinates, all tails, and the mean correction. Retaining the original fourth-stage witness margin gives a stronger conditional comparison. A completed fourth-stage recomputation uses repaired integer-power bounds and fresh exact acceptance; historical same-code replays retain a separate arithmetic qualification. We prove positivity for arbitrarily many sufficiently separated logarithmic-complexity windows and for the entire actual zero-jet space in a single band of width eleven times the logarithm of height. A complete interaction estimate also permits sparse families of entire growing bands under the stated stronger spacing rule. For a fixed low space with a finite theta exponential-polynomial representation, the exact response cost of the entire sparse high family tends to zero in its actual weighted Gram. Separately replayed ball-arithmetic certificates supply positive margins on the first four and five exact zero modes, with their respective weighted coupling constants. The fifth-mode proof retains a derivative Gram and verifies its complete shifted Schur cost. Derivative-Gram graph bounds transfer that five-mode certificate, and then an incremental six-mode certificate, to their Gamma-low projections together with the complete complementary Gamma-high form domain under explicit sector and operator-row inputs, while leaving the other low directions unresolved. Under the stated sparse estimates, the four-mode low space attaches to an infinite selected tail above an explicit, very large height. A separate fixed-real-lattice theorem permits unrestricted finite or infinite mode count, and a signed block criterion identifies the additional coupling requirements. A restricted Gamma spectral decomposition on the actual zero-jet sector gives projected graph cores and an exact low-space Schur criterion. Fixed inverse filters, complete residual optimization, exact null-response repair, and ordered tail transfer clarify the further obligations of a cofinal certificate. Uniform two-sided operator brackets separate response accuracy from source dimension, while an asymptotic obstruction shows why a fixed graph debit cannot certify an exhaustive raw-source family. Each result states its norm, domain, geometry, and verification boundary. The qualitative prime-number-theorem refinement supplies no effective final height here. These restricted positivity results do not establish positivity on arbitrary unions or the full zero family. The global half inequality and the Riemann hypothesis remain unproved.
