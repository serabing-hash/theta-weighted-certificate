# Actual Gamma-sector bridge coverage

The monograph integrates the unique restricted-operator bridge after the actual zero-jet sign equivalence. Its proofs use the previously established actual reducing sector and full-zero-jet form and graph cores. It supplies no new actual theta sign or numerical global coefficient.

| Component | Coverage and required distinction |
| --- | --- |
| Restricted Gamma form | A closed form on the actual reducing sector, with representative Gamma_K and exact domain. Ambient Gamma invariance is not presumed. |
| Projected graph cores | Spectral projection of the admitted jet core gives a graph core for the positive high operator and its bounded perturbations. Projected jets need not be finite original-jet combinations. |
| High response bounds | For a spectral cut R greater than the bounded correction norm, the high block has gap R-d and both absolute inverse-response bounds hold. Infinite orthogonal rows are positive strong sums. |
| Exact Schur criterion | Square completion on the entire form domain proves necessity and sufficiency on the whole low Gamma spectral space. The low projection is not assumed finite rank. |
| Complete prime debit | The cutoff error pays the low block, both response factors and the inverse correction. All full weighted residual rows and the actual Gram remain present. |
| Cofinal criterion | Nested low spaces, vanishing Gram-relative tolerances and separately chosen cutoff and projected-jet responses at every stage. The converse pays twice the prime error and the complete residual debit. No uniform rate in dimension is claimed. |
| Scope | Gamma spectral spaces are not zero-height windows, coordinate deletions, raw compact sources or fixed-filter images. Absolute response smallness does not prove the low sign or kernel compatibility. |

The original seven-page report's other substantive components are already proved in the monograph: the ambient jet graph core, actual sign equivalence, signed infinite-block criterion, fixed inverse and full residual methods, and ordered complete tails. They are reused through internal references instead of duplicated. The unique restricted-form and projected-response proofs are included in full.

Three exact rational structural examples were replayed. They check algebraic identities and sufficient-versus-necessary distinctions, not actual theta matrices. The bridge has no bearing on the independent Decimal response-source revalidation. The remaining actual Schur sign, every cofinal sign, global half inequality and RH remain unproved.
