The earlier four-zero result remains separately available. This is a finite-space extension, not an all-dimension sign theorem.

## Reproduction boundary

The source replay regenerated the frozen four-mode block and reproduced its mathematical fields. The five-mode checkpoint, final rational matrix and exact acceptance record reproduced byte for byte. Higher-precision runs retained the same final rational matrix. Live-object serialization checks covered 225 matrix entries and all 25 final entry boxes. A separate rational determinant calculation checked the finite signs and normalized reserve. These layers are distinct from a second full quadrature implementation or formal verification.

The finite certificate uses Python-FLINT 0.9.0 and FLINT 3.6.0 Arb/Acb. It is independent of the historical Decimal integer-power issue in the global response stages and does not repair that issue.
