# Changelog

## Manuscript v1.0 - November 1, 2026

This version is a substantive mathematical revision of the v0.1 analytical preprint draft. The original v0.1 files are preserved separately and unchanged.

### The former bridge assumption is proved

- Replaced the compact physical bridge hypothesis (H) by a theorem with a complete proof.
- Stated the classical Guinand explicit formula as an external theorem and derived the manuscript's Gamma kernel, constant, prime coefficients, and pole coefficient in its precise Fourier convention.
- Derived the entire theta transform from the Jacobi transformation and a Mellin integral; every nontrivial zeta zero is a transform zero without assuming that it lies on the critical line.
- Proved the weak theta source equation through compact cutoffs. The proof separately handles Gamma-form convergence, moments, complete prime pairings, and the entire zero sum using a uniform strip bound and the classical zero-counting estimate.
- Included both endpoint terms in the ground-state calculation, with convergence established before subtracting terms.
- Removed conditional language only from results whose dependence on the former (H) is now discharged. The proof uses classical explicit-formula input, not a zero-location or positivity assumption.

### The exact essential threshold is established

- Proved upper bounds for every theta derivative and a positive first-summand lower bound for the theta kernel.
- Used these bounds to place even theta derivatives in the exact physical form domain.
- Extended the weak explicit-formula null identity to those derivatives and then to the full physical form domain.
- Constructed independent centered vectors `g_j = kappa^(2j)/kappa - 4^(-j)` in the original operator domain, all with eigenvalue 1/2.
- Combined their infinite multiplicity with fixed-distance spectral finiteness to establish the essential spectral bottom exactly at 1/2.

### Formal manuscript presentation

- Updated the abstract, principal theorem, logical dependencies, and conclusion to match the proved results.
- Retained the complete maximal-domain, core, weighted-prime-tail, compactness, eigenstate-correspondence, negative-index, and mean-domain proofs.
- Added explicit external references for the classical explicit formula, zero-counting estimate, and digamma integral.
- Preserved the author's name, affiliation, and ORCID; no email address, private paths, conversation identifiers, or references to the author's prior papers appear in the manuscript.
- Changed the version label to Manuscript v1.0. This does not claim journal acceptance, peer review, historical priority, or proof-assistant verification.

### What remains unresolved

The paper does not exclude centered eigenstates below 1/2, prove strict positivity of the second pencil eigenvalue for every parameter in (0, 1/2), establish the global variance half bound, or prove the Riemann hypothesis. It does not assert that the derivative family is complete or construct the ordinary-L2 pencil at parameter zero. No numerical research or sign certification is included.
