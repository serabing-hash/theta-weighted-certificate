At B=1 the two polynomial certificates used the same implementation in recorded separate replays; the adapted 22-dimensional Schur matrix remains undecided. At B=2 the stored interval calculation covers only a specified two-dimensional low plane and its designated high complement. The 74,998 remaining low directions and their couplings were not certified. Scientific calculations were not rerun as part of V6 manuscript preparation.
